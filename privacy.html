Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Remry collects, why, where it is kept, and how you can export or delete it. It is written to be accurate to how the Remry app works today. Last updated 13 November 2026.

1. Who we are

Remry (“we”, “us”) is operated by Prabhdeep Aulakh (PRABHDEEP AULAKH). Remry is a personal reminder and memory app for iPhone and iPad. We are based in Australia and handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

The app is published under the name Remry – Your Second Memory. For privacy questions, email remrysupport@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

You can use Remry as a guest, without creating a named account. If you choose Sign in with Apple, we receive the Apple account identifier, and — only if you share them — your name and email address. We do not receive your Apple password.

On our servers we keep:

  • Your account identifier, sign-in session, and settings you choose in Remry (such as time zone, default currency, whether notifications are on, and whether you have agreed to use cloud AI).
  • The memories and reminders you save: titles, notes, categories, dates and times, and any text that was read from a photo or a spoken note so it can be found later.
  • A short record of questions you ask Remry and the answers given, so we can show your conversation and improve those answers for you.
  • A simple count of cloud AI use, so free and optional paid limits can be applied when those limits are switched on.

We do not store the actual photo, PDF or voice-recording files on our servers. Those stay on your iPhone or iPad. The server may keep a small reference that a file exists on your device.

We do not use advertising trackers, and we have not built analytics or crash-reporting SDKs into Remry. We do not sell your personal information.

3. What stays on your device

These are saved in Remry’s storage on your iPhone or iPad, not on Remry’s servers:

  • Photos, PDFs and documents you attach to memories or to the vault.
  • Your document vault, cards and vouchers, bills and renewals, shopping list, medicine list, bin schedule, “where is it?” notes, and the parked-car spot (including the location used for that spot).
  • The reminder notifications scheduled on the device, so they can still appear when Remry is closed.

If you use iCloud or computer backups for this device, those on-device files may be included in your own backup. Remry does not operate a separate cloud photo album.

4. AI, voice, photos, camera, microphone and location

Voice and microphone

If you allow the microphone and Speech Recognition, you can speak a memory or a question. Remry turns your speech into text on your device using Apple’s on-device speech recognition. That text is then treated like something you typed.

Only if cloud AI is available, you have turned it on in Remry, and on-device speech could not be used, a short recording may be sent to our cloud AI provider so it can be turned into text. The recording is read once and is not kept on Remry’s servers.

Camera and photos

If you allow the camera or photo library, you can photograph or pick a receipt, letter, card or document. Remry first tries to read the words on your device. If that is not enough, and you have agreed to cloud AI, the file may be sent once to our servers to be read, then discarded. It is not stored on the server.

Cloud AI

Cloud AI is off until you agree to it in Remry. When it is on, the text you submit (and, only if needed, a photo or a short recording) may be sent to our cloud AI provider — currently OpenAI — so Remry can suggest titles, dates and answers. We ask you to confirm suggested reminders before they are saved. Please do not include information in a prompt that you would not want processed by that provider.

Location

Remry uses location only while you are using the app, and only when you ask it to remember where you parked. It looks up the spot once, saves it on your device, and does not track you in the background. The parked-car location is not sent to Remry’s servers.

Face ID

If you turn on a Remry lock or open the document vault, your device may ask for Face ID or your passcode. Face data is handled by Apple on your device. Remry never receives a scan of your face.

Notifications

Reminder alerts are scheduled as local notifications on your device from the plan Remry prepares. You can turn notifications off in Remry Settings or in the iOS Settings app.

5. Subscriptions and payments

Remry is free to use, with optional Remry Plus. If you subscribe, Apple processes the payment through your Apple ID. We do not receive your full card number.

To know whether your subscription is active, Remry is designed to use RevenueCat, which reads the subscription status provided by Apple and links it to your Remry account. You can restore purchases in the app with the same Apple ID.

6. How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • provide Remry — saving, reminding, searching, asking, and showing your Home screen;
  • keep you signed in, and merge a guest session into a Sign in with Apple account if you choose to sign in later;
  • schedule and display reminders;
  • honour your settings, including AI consent and notification preferences;
  • help you export or delete your data when you ask;
  • respond to a support email you send us;
  • protect the service (for example, basic rate limits) and comply with the law.

We do not use your memories or reminders to advertise to you, and we do not sell them.

7. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only as needed to run Remry:

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, App Store payments and, on your device, speech, Face ID, notifications, camera, photos and location.
  • Our hosting providers — the Remry servers and database that store the account, memory and reminder information described above, solely to host the service for us.
  • OpenAI — only when you have agreed to cloud AI, and only the text or file needed for that request.
  • RevenueCat — subscription status, if you use Remry Plus.
  • You, or someone you ask us to help — if you email support, or if the law requires us to disclose information.

8. Where it is stored and how long we keep it

Account, memory and reminder information is stored on Remry’s servers so it can appear in the app and, if you sign in with Apple, move with you to a new device. Those servers may be located outside Australia. On-device files and lists stay on your iPhone or iPad.

We keep this information until you delete a single item, or until you use Delete all my data, which removes your account and the server records we hold for you. Guest sessions that are no longer in use and are older than a day may be removed automatically.

If you email us, we keep the email long enough to help you and to keep a record of the request.

9. Your rights, export and deletion

Under the Australian Privacy Principles you may ask to access or correct the personal information we hold, and you may complain if you believe we have mishandled it. You can also:

  • edit or delete an individual memory or reminder in the app;
  • tap Export my data in Settings, to receive a file of what Remry holds for you;
  • tap Delete all my data in Settings, which permanently deletes your account and server data, and the Remry files, lists and notifications on that device. This cannot be undone.

If you cannot open the app, email remrysupport@gmail.com and we will help with access, correction or deletion. We may need to confirm that the request comes from you.

10. Children

Remry is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please email remrysupport@gmail.com and we will delete it.

11. Contact and complaints

Privacy questions and requests: remrysupport@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 1–2 business days (Australia time).

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

We may update this policy if Remry changes. The “last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of Remry after an update means the new policy applies from that date. If a change is significant, we will also describe it in the app or on this site where we reasonably can.
