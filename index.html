What Remry does

Remry is mainly a reminder and notification app, and also a private memory assistant. It is designed to be simple, readable and gentle to use.

✦

Just Tell Remry

Say or type one sentence. Remry can turn it into several reminders, and shows you a confirmation before anything is saved.

◎

Never miss a day

Birthdays, bills and renewals, medicine, bins, parking and the morning brief — with notifications that still work when Remry is closed.

❀

Ask what you saved

Ask Remry a question and it answers only from what you have already saved, with the source so you can open the exact item.

Everyday help, kept simple

Reminders that find you

Voice or text, one-time or repeating. Today on the Home screen keeps what is due until you mark it done or delete it yourself.

Bills and renewals

Photograph a bill or enter it yourself. Remry helps you remember due dates, and you can mark a bill as paid when it is done.

A private Face ID vault

Keep IDs, cards and papers behind Face ID on your device. Photos and documents stay on your iPhone or iPad.

Your data, your choice

Export what Remry holds for you, or delete your account and data from Settings. You can also sign in with Apple if you want your memories to come with you to a new phone.

Remry is free to use, with optional Remry Plus. We do not list prices here, because they are not final.

Need a hand?

The Support page has short answers for notifications, permissions, Face ID, export, deleting your data and subscriptions. Our help line is email support — we aim to reply within 1–2 business days.
