Support and Help

Short answers to the questions people ask most. If you still need help, email remrysupport@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 1–2 business days (Australia time).

I see a notification on the lock screen, but no banner

This is normal iPhone behaviour. When your iPhone or iPad is locked, Remry shows the reminder on the lock screen and can play a sound. Banners appear when the device is unlocked, if banners are allowed for Remry.

To check: open the iOS Settings app, tap Notifications, then Remry. Turn on Allow Notifications, and choose Lock Screen, Banner and Sound as you prefer. Also check that a Focus mode (such as Do Not Disturb or Sleep) is not hiding Remry.

Reminders are not showing at all

Please check all of these:

  • In the iOS Settings app, Notifications → Remry, Allow Notifications is on.
  • In Remry, open Settings and make sure reminder notifications are turned on.
  • The reminder was confirmed and saved (Just Tell Remry does not save anything until you tap Confirm).
  • Your iPhone is not in Silent mode if you expected a sound, and Focus is not silencing Remry.

If you have just reinstalled Remry, open the app once so it can set up your reminders again.

How do I turn on the permissions Remry needs?

Open the iOS Settings app, scroll to Remry, and you can allow:

  • Notifications — so reminders can reach you when the app is closed.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition — so you can speak instead of typing. Speech is turned into text on your device.
  • Camera and Photos — only when you choose to photograph or pick a receipt, letter, card or document.
  • Face ID — to lock Remry or your document vault.
  • Location (While Using the App) — only to save where you parked, when you ask Remry to remember it. Remry does not track you in the background.

You can leave any of these off. The features that need them simply will not be available until you allow them.

How does Face ID work in Remry?

In Remry Settings you can turn on Lock with Face ID. Remry will then ask for Face ID (or your device passcode) when you open the app.

The document vault also asks for Face ID each time you open it, so IDs and papers stay private even if the rest of Remry is unlocked.

If Face ID is not set up, add it in the iOS Settings app under Face ID & Passcode first. Remry uses the Face ID already on your device. We never see your face data.

How do I export my data?

In Remry, open Settings and tap Export my data. Remry prepares a file you can share or save. It includes the memories, reminders and account information held for you, plus the everyday lists kept on your device (such as bills, shopping and vault items).

Photos and documents themselves live in Remry’s folder on your device (and in your iPhone backup, if you use one). They are not stored on Remry’s servers, so the export file describes them rather than attaching every photo.

How do I delete my account and all my data?

In Remry, open Settings and tap Delete all my data. Remry will ask you to confirm. This permanently removes your account, memories and reminders from Remry’s servers, and deletes the photos, files and lists saved on this device, including scheduled notifications. It cannot be undone.

If you signed in with Apple, Remry also asks Apple to revoke that sign-in when it can. You can also stop the app’s access in iOS Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple.

If you cannot open the app, email remrysupport@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will help you complete the deletion.

Subscriptions and restoring purchases

Remry is free to use, with optional Remry Plus. If you subscribe, Apple handles the payment through your Apple ID. You can change or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

If you already paid on this or another device with the same Apple ID, open Remry and tap Restore purchases on the Remry Plus screen. We never ask you to email a password or card number.

I tapped a notification and Remry did not show the reminder

Please install the latest version of Remry, then try again. A reminder stays in Today on the Home screen until you mark it done or swipe to delete it. It should not disappear on its own.

If it still opens empty, take a note of the reminder name and time and email us at remrysupport@gmail.com.

How do I contact Remry?

Email our help line at remrysupport@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 1–2 business days, Australia time. We do not currently offer a phone number.

Please include the device you use (for example iPhone 15 or iPad) and a short description of what happened. Do not send passwords, full ID numbers or card numbers.
